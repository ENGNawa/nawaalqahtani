Overview

Damanak is a digital warranty management platform that helps users store and organize product warranties, invoices and purchase information in one centralized place.

The Problem

Warranty information is often scattered across paper receipts, photos, files and emails, making it difficult to access documents and track warranty status.

I designed and developed Damanak as a centralized system for managing products, warranties, invoices and warranty status.
